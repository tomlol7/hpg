About Human Phenotypes

Understanding the diversity of human features

A phenotype refers to the observable physical characteristics of an individual, including facial structure, hair type, skin tone, eye color, and other visible traits. These features are influenced by both genetics and environment, and they vary widely across populations worldwide.

This website analyzes your facial features from a selfie and compares them to documented anthropological types on Human Phenotypes. By doing so, it finds the closest matches to your phenotype. The analysis is fully local — your photo never leaves your device.

Why Phenotypes Matter

Studying human phenotypes helps scientists understand patterns of genetic variation, evolutionary adaptations, and the rich diversity of human populations. While two individuals may share similar traits, each person’s combination of features is unique, shaped by ancestry and environment.

Privacy and Safety

Your privacy is our priority. All image processing occurs in your browser. We do not store, upload, or share your photos in any way. The results you see are entirely local and private to your device.

Educational Purpose

This tool is designed for educational and entertainment purposes. It provides an approximation of your phenotype type based on facial features. It is not a definitive measurement of ancestry, ethnicity, or personal identity.
